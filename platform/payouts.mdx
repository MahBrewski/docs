Payouts are requested from the dashboard and settled on-chain, directly to your embedded wallet.

## How payouts work

<Steps>
  <Step title="Trade an eligible account">
    Stay within your rules and payout conditions.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Reach eligibility">
    Eligibility depends on your account type and any add-ons.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Request the payout">
    Submit the request from the dashboard.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Settled">
    The platform verifies your performance and settles the funds to your wallet.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What payouts depend on

| Factor              | Why it matters                                |
| ------------------- | --------------------------------------------- |
| **Account type**    | Each type has its own split and conditions    |
| **Add-ons**         | The 80% or 90% split changes your percentage  |
| **Rule compliance** | The account must remain in good standing      |
| **Funded conditions**| Your account's specific conditions must be met |

## Withdrawal conditions

| Account type               | Requirement                      |
| -------------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| **Instant Funding** *(soon)* | First withdrawal after 5% profit |
| **1-Step**                 | At least 1% account profit       |
| **2-Step Elite**           | At least 1% account profit       |
| **2-Step Classic**         | At least 1% account profit       |

## Before requesting, confirm

* The account is eligible and funded.
* No rules have been breached.
* The wallet destination is correct.
* Your profit threshold is met.
* Your add-on configuration matches the split you expect.
