Eligible payouts are requested from the dashboard and settled through on-chain infrastructure.

## How payouts work

<Steps>
  <Step title="Trade an eligible account">
    Trade your funded account while staying within the applicable account rules and payout conditions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reach payout eligibility">
    Eligibility depends on your account type and any selected add-ons.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Request payout from the dashboard">
    When the account becomes eligible, submit the payout request from the dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Receive settlement">
    The platform verifies performance and sends funds to your wallet through its settlement flow.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What payouts depend on

Payout percentages depend on:

- your account type
- your selected add-ons

Payout access also depends on remaining in good standing under the account's rules.

## What affects payout eligibility

- your account type
- your account's rule compliance
- any selected add-ons
- the funded stage conditions attached to the account

## Withdrawal conditions

| Account type | Withdrawal requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Degen (Instant Funding) | First withdrawal after 5% profit |
| 1-Step | Minimum 1% account profit |
| 2-Step Elite | Minimum 1% account profit |
| 2-Step Classic | Minimum 1% account profit |

## Wallet destination

Payout settlement goes to the embedded wallet created for your DojiFunded account.

Payouts are a direct user-facing part of the funded trading journey, not a separate manual back-office process.

## What users should confirm before requesting a payout

Before requesting a payout, confirm:

- the account is in an eligible funded state
- account rules have not been breached
- the wallet destination is correct
- any account-specific payout thresholds have been met
- the selected add-on configuration still matches the expected profit split

<Info>
  Payouts are designed to be instant and on-chain. If payout timing or destination rules change, update this page with the current platform behavior.
</Info>
