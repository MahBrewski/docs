Eligible payouts are requested from the dashboard and settled through on-chain infrastructure.

## How payouts work

<Steps>
  <Step title={<span style={{color: "#B7FF00"}}>Trade an eligible account</span>}>
    Trade your funded account while staying within the applicable account rules and payout conditions.
  </Step>
  <Step title={<span style={{color: "#B7FF00"}}>Reach payout eligibility</span>}>
    Eligibility depends on your account type and any selected add-ons.
  </Step>
  <Step title={<span style={{color: "#B7FF00"}}>Request payout from the dashboard</span>}>
    When the account becomes eligible, submit the payout request from the dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title={<span style={{color: "#B7FF00"}}>Receive settlement</span>}>
    The platform verifies performance and sends funds to your wallet through its settlement flow.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What payouts depend on

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card icon="scale-balanced" title="Account type">Each account type has its own profit split and withdrawal conditions</Card>
  <Card icon="sliders" title="Selected add-ons">Add-ons such as 80% or 90% profit split affect your payout percentage</Card>
  <Card icon="shield-check" title="Rule compliance">Account must remain in good standing under all active rules</Card>
  <Card icon="circle-check" title="Funded stage conditions">Conditions attached to your specific account must be met</Card>
</CardGroup>

## Withdrawal conditions

| Account type | Withdrawal requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Instant Funding (coming soon) | First withdrawal after 5% profit |
| 1-Step | Minimum 1% account profit |
| 2-Step Elite | Minimum 1% account profit |
| 2-Step Classic | Minimum 1% account profit |

## Wallet destination

Payout settlement goes to the embedded wallet created for your DojiFunded account.

Payouts are a direct user-facing part of the funded trading journey, not a separate manual back-office process.

## Before requesting a payout, confirm

<CardGroup cols={1}>
  <Card icon="circle-check">The account is in an eligible funded state</Card>
  <Card icon="shield-halved">Account rules have not been breached</Card>
  <Card icon="wallet">The wallet destination is correct</Card>
  <Card icon="chart-line">Account-specific payout thresholds have been met</Card>
  <Card icon="sliders">The selected add-on configuration matches the expected profit split</Card>
</CardGroup>

<Info>
