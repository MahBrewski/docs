The AI copilot is the natural-language assistance layer inside DojiFunded.

It supports AI-native workflows for modern traders and autonomous strategy users.

## What to expect

The current product direction points to a copilot surface that helps users:

- interact with trading workflows in natural language
- review account context
- work more efficiently inside the platform

## Current live workflow

The live app currently exposes this experience through the `Agents` route.

The current interface suggests a conversation-based workflow where users can:

- ask trading and market questions
- analyze positions and PnL
- interact with account-aware context
- trigger assistant-guided trading workflows

The UI also suggests persistent conversations rather than a single temporary prompt box.
