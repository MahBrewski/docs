The DojiFunded Explorer helps you inspect trade positions that are recorded on-chain as NFTs.

It connects funded-phase trading activity to a verifiable on-chain record.

## What the Explorer shows

Users should expect Explorer views to surface:

- trade NFT identifiers
- position metadata such as symbol, side, size, and leverage
- timestamps for opened and closed positions
- account or challenge references where available
- links out to the relevant chain explorer

## How it fits the platform

The frontend route is an `NFT Explorer` experience connected to your authenticated account.

It is designed to help you:

- confirm ownership of trade NFTs
- inspect funded-phase position history
- cross-check trade records against on-chain data

## What to expect today

The live app currently exposes an authenticated Explorer flow rather than a public browse-all directory.

Users sign in, review their trade NFT inventory, and open position details from there.

<Info>
  Explorer coverage should stay aligned with the live `/explore` product route and the on-chain records available for each account.
</Info>
