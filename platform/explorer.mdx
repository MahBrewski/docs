## What each trade page shows

| Field               | What it reports                       |
| ------------------- | ------------------------------------- |
| **Execution prices**| Requested and actual execution price  |
| **Realised PnL**    | Final profit or loss on closed positions |
| **Fees & funding**  | Full breakdown of trading and funding costs |
| **Leverage & size** | Exact leverage and position sizing    |
| **Trade duration**  | Open and close timestamps             |
| **Rule tracking**   | Whether the trade stayed within account rules |
| **On-chain proof**  | Blockchain record tied to the trade   |

## Execution transparency

Because the Explorer shows both the requested and the actual execution price, you get full visibility into how each trade filled.

| Metric               | What it means                          |
| -------------------- | -------------------------------------- |
| **Slippage**         | Difference between requested and filled price |
| **Market impact**    | How position size affected the fill    |
| **Execution quality**| How closely the fill matched the order |
| **Volatility at fill**| Market conditions at the moment of execution |

## On-chain verification

Every trade carries identifiers you can check independently:

* Contract address
* Position ID
* Trade ID
* NFT token ID
* Blockchain proof on Arbiscan

The terminal is your live interface; the Explorer is the permanent public record. Between them, you verify your own activity rather than relying on opaque internal reporting.
