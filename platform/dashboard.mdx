The dashboard is the control center for your DojiFunded account.

## What the dashboard shows

- active and completed accounts
- payout history and payout eligibility
- performance metrics
- PnL and account analytics
- risk statistics
- account progress across evaluation and funded stages

## How traders use the dashboard

Use the dashboard to:

- monitor your current account state
- review funded account progress
- manage wallet and payout actions
- access the trading terminal

## How the dashboard connects to the rest of the platform

Trading happens in the terminal, but the dashboard is where you review the broader account picture.

This is also where user-facing payout actions and account history come together.
