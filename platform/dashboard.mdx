The dashboard is the control center for your DojiFunded account.

## What the dashboard shows

- active and completed accounts
- payout history and payout eligibility
- performance metrics
- PnL and account analytics
- risk statistics
- account progress across evaluation and funded stages

## Typical dashboard sections

The current product direction suggests the dashboard brings together:

- account overview
- challenge or funded account progress
- performance metrics and analytics
- payout state and payout history
- wallet-linked actions
- account history across evaluations and funded accounts

## How traders use the dashboard

Use the dashboard to:

- monitor your current account state
- review funded account progress
- manage wallet and payout actions
- access the trading terminal

## How the dashboard fits the lifecycle

The dashboard is where the user can step back from live order entry and review the broader account picture.

That includes:

- whether an account is still inside its rules
- whether a funded account is approaching payout eligibility
- how performance has changed over time
- which accounts are active, completed, or funded

## How the dashboard connects to the rest of the platform

Trading happens in the terminal, but the dashboard is where you review the broader account picture.

This is also where user-facing payout actions and account history come together.

Performance data is also part of the wider on-chain trading record described in the product brief, including trade-position NFT tracking.
