## What it's for

* Test your strategy logic against historical data.
* Review how a strategy behaves before deploying it.
* Support AI-native workflows more broadly.

## Current workflow

The live app exposes a backtesting workspace built around **saved sessions**. You can:

* Create a new session and name it.
* Choose a starting balance.
* Choose a historical date range.
* Open the session workspace for deeper analysis.

Backtesting is a persistent list of sessions, not a one-off calculator.
