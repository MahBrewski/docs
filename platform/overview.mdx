- institutional-grade trading infrastructure
- multi-asset market access
- a trader dashboard with account analytics
- on-chain execution and verification
- integrated risk engine enforcement
- an embedded trading wallet
- AI-native tools
- instant on-chain payouts for eligible accounts

## Who the platform is built for

The platform is designed for:

- funded traders
- systematic strategies
- autonomous agents operating under defined rules
