The terminal is the main trading surface in DojiFunded.

It is where you place trades, manage positions, and monitor account state in real time.

## What you can do in the terminal

- select markets and execution venues
- view chart data and current pricing
- submit market, limit, and managed orders where supported
- monitor open positions, orders, fills, and funding history
- track risk alerts and trading availability

## Core terminal areas

The terminal is built around a few dense working areas:

- a charting area for market context
- an order entry panel for trade execution
- account-aware market and account selection
- bottom panels for positions, orders, trade history, position history, and funding history
- rule and market-status notices that can affect order entry

## What the terminal is tied to

The terminal is not a detached trading screen.

It is tied to:

- your selected or active challenge account
- the execution venue behind that account
- the product's rule and risk model
- market availability and trading status

## How the terminal fits the account model

The terminal is tied to your active account and account rules.

That means the platform can enforce:

- drawdown limits
- risk-per-trade rules
- minimum trade duration
- account-specific restrictions

Trading availability can change based on asset class, market status, and account-specific constraints.

## What you can monitor while trading

Inside the terminal, users should expect visibility into:

- current positions
- open orders
- trade history
- position history
- funding events
- market-level pricing and book context
- account health and risk state

## What to expect while trading

- account status updates in real time
- performance and positions sync into the dashboard
- rule compliance is checked continuously
- off-platform execution is not part of the funded workflow

<Info>
  The terminal is the default destination in the product. It is the center of the trading workflow, not an optional advanced page.
</Info>
