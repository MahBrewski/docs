The terminal is the main trading surface in DojiFunded.

It is where you place trades, manage positions, and monitor account state in real time.

## What you can do in the terminal

- select markets and execution venues
- view chart data and current pricing
- submit market, limit, and managed orders where supported
- monitor open positions, orders, fills, and funding history
- track risk alerts and trading availability

## How the terminal fits the account model

The terminal is tied to your active account and account rules.

That means the platform can enforce:

- drawdown limits
- risk-per-trade rules
- minimum trade duration
- account-specific restrictions

## What to expect while trading

- account status updates in real time
- performance and positions sync into the dashboard
- rule compliance is checked continuously
- off-platform execution is not part of the funded workflow
