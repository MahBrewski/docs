The terminal is a **multi-asset trading environment** covering crypto, forex, commodities, equities, and more, in a single workspace.

Every trade routes through the integrated on-chain venues, currently **GMX** and **Ostium**, and executes against live market conditions. Real prices, real liquidity, real spreads. Alongside that, you get full charting, position management, live PnL, and integrated risk monitoring.

## Order types

| Order type     | Trigger                 | Execution                        |
| -------------- | ----------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| **Market**     | Immediate               | Fills against available liquidity |
| **Limit**      | Price reaches your limit | Fills at your limit or better    |
| **Stop market**| Trigger price reached   | Converts into a market order     |
| **Stop limit** | Trigger price reached   | Places a limit order             |

Your final fill price may move with market conditions, liquidity, and order size.

## Margin modes

Every position uses **isolated margin**. Cross margin is coming soon. Maximum leverage varies by market and venue, shown next to each pair in the terminal.

## Market data

Live prices stream directly from the venues, driving your fills, PnL, drawdown monitoring, and funding or rollover calculations. On supported perpetual markets, funding fees accrue hourly.

## Risk overlay

A live risk panel updates while your positions are open. Because drawdown limits are measured on **realised balance**, open positions show here as unrealized PnL but do not count against your drawdown until you close them.

| Metric                     | What it shows                                |
| -------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| **Current drawdown**       | Drawdown usage against your limit, on realised balance |
| **Daily loss usage**       | Portion of today's daily loss allowance used |
| **Margin utilization**     | Margin used across open positions            |
| **Unrealized PnL**         | Live profit or loss on open positions        |
| **Funding & holding costs**| Funding fees accruing on perpetuals          |
