The DojiFunded terminal is a multi-asset trading environment designed for funded traders across crypto, forex, commodities, equities, and additional supported markets.

All trades are routed through integrated on-chain execution venues including [GMX](https://gmx.io) and [Ostium](https://ostium.io). Execution is simulated against live market conditions, liquidity, spreads, and venue-specific pricing models.

The terminal includes full charting, position management, real-time PnL tracking, and integrated risk monitoring.

## Order Types

| Order Type   | Trigger               | Execution                          |
| :----------: | :-------------------: | :--------------------------------: |
| Market       | Immediate             | Executes against available liquidity |
| Limit        | Price reaches limit   | Executes at limit price or better  |
| Stop Market  | Trigger price reached | Converts into market order         |
| Stop Limit   | Trigger price reached | Places a limit order               |

Market conditions, liquidity, and execution impact may affect final fill prices depending on venue conditions and order size.

## Margin Modes

| Setting      | Options       |
| :----------: | :-----------: |
| Margin Mode  | Isolated      |
| Cross Margin | Coming Soon   |

Each position currently operates using isolated margin. Maximum leverage varies by market and execution venue, with supported leverage displayed directly next to each trading pair inside the terminal.

## Market Data

DojiFunded streams live market data directly from integrated execution venues. Real-time pricing is used for order execution, PnL calculations, drawdown monitoring, and funding or rollover calculations.

Execution conditions dynamically account for liquidity, volatility, and position size to simulate realistic trading environments. Additional funding fees may accrue hourly on supported perpetual markets.

## Risk Overlay

The terminal includes a live risk overlay displaying:

- current drawdown
- daily loss usage
- margin utilization
- unrealized PnL
- funding and holding costs

Risk metrics update continuously while positions remain open.

## PnL Share Cards

Generate and share visual trading performance cards directly from the dashboard. PnL cards include profit and loss metrics, trading statistics, account performance, and payout information designed for public profiles and social sharing.
