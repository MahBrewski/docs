Your full path, from the landing page to your first payout.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Landing">
    See how DojiFunded works and which account types are available before you begin.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Sign up and login">
    Sign up with email or wallet. Each user has a single trading identity, so a connected wallet is mandatory and handles execution, verification, and payouts.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Account selection">
    Each type carries fixed parameters set in advance: profit targets, drawdown limits, payout conditions, and trading restrictions. Add-ons can adjust some parameters, such as your profit split.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Payment">
    A single fee based on the account type. As soon as it confirms, the account activates. No recurring fees.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Trading">
    All trading happens inside the platform. Each account gets a virtual allocation, for example $10,000.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Monitoring">
    The platform tracks positions, drawdown, on-chain records, and rule compliance in real time.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Payouts">
    Meet the conditions, request a withdrawal, get verified on-chain, and receive funds directly to your wallet. Your payout percentage depends on account type and add-ons.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What the virtual balance is and isn't

* The balance is **not withdrawable**.
* It exists only for internal accounting and risk logic.
* Trading behaviour mirrors real market conditions.
* All trades execute through your connected wallet. Off-platform trading is not permitted.

## What's tracked while you trade

| Signal              | What is tracked                                             |
| ------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Positions & PnL** | Live position and profit or loss monitoring                |
| **Drawdowns**       | Drawdown usage, measured on HWM basis                      |
| **On-chain records**| Positions recorded on-chain as trade NFTs with full metadata |
| **Rule compliance** | Checked continuously throughout trading                    |

All of it is visible in the dashboard and the Explorer. Any attempt to bypass the rules, such as wash trading or off-platform execution, invalidates the account.
