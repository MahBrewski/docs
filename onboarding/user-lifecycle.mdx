This page explains how users move through DojiFunded from account setup to funded trading and payouts.

## 1. Landing page

Users start on the DojiFunded landing page where they can:

- Understand how DojiFunded works
- Review available account types
- Begin the onboarding process

## 2. Sign up and login

Users create an account using one of three methods:

- `Email` — sign up with your email address
- `Wallet` — connect an existing wallet
- `Social handle` — optional social login (X)

Each user is associated with a single trading identity. A connected wallet is mandatory and is used for trade execution, account verification, and payouts.

## 3. Account selection

After logging in, users select one of the following account types:

- `Instant` — instant-funded account, no evaluation required
- `1-Step Evaluation` — single-phase evaluation to qualify for funding
- `2-Step Evaluation` — two-phase evaluation with structured targets

Each account type has fixed parameters defined in advance: profit targets, drawdown limits, payout conditions, and trading restrictions. Optional add-ons may adjust certain parameters such as profit split or hedging permissions.

## 4. Payment

Users pay a one-time fee based on the selected account type via:

- `Crypto` — supported cryptocurrency (USDC on Arbitrum)
- `Card` — debit or credit card (coming soon)

Once payment is confirmed, the account is activated and the user gains access to the trading platform. There are no recurring fees for evaluations.

## 5. Trading

All trading takes place inside the DojiFunded platform. Each account is assigned a virtual allocation, for example `$10,000`:

- The balance is not withdrawable
- Exists only for internal accounting and risk logic
- Trading behaviour mirrors real market conditions

All trades must be executed using the connected wallet. Off-platform trading is not permitted.

## 6. Monitoring and verification

While trading, the platform tracks everything in real time:

- `Positions & PnL` — live position tracking and profit/loss monitoring
- `Drawdowns` — continuous drawdown usage monitoring
- `On-chain NFTs` — trade positions recorded on-chain with full position metadata
- `Rule compliance` — checked continuously throughout trading

Performance data is visible in the dashboard and the trade NFT Explorer. Any attempt to bypass platform rules — including wash trading, prohibited strategies, or external execution — invalidates the account.

## 7. Payouts

When payout conditions are met:

- The trader requests a withdrawal from the dashboard
- Performance is verified on-chain
- Funds are sent directly to the trader's wallet

Payout percentages depend on the account type and any selected add-ons.
