This page explains how users move through DojiFunded from account setup to funded trading and payouts.

## Lifecycle stages

### 1. Entry and sign-up

Users can discover DojiFunded through the website, referral links, or direct links into the app.

The live frontend routes the root app path to the trading terminal on `/trade`.

From there, users can:

- sign up
- review account options
- move into the trading workflow

### 2. Sign up and login

Users can create an account with:

- email
- wallet connection

Each user has one trading identity.

During signup, DojiFunded creates an embedded wallet for the account.

The connected wallet and embedded wallet support flows such as:

- login and verification
- payments and settlement
- payouts

### 3. Account selection

After login, users choose an account type and an execution venue.

These choices determine:

- supported trading pairs
- fee structure and funding costs
- execution environment and liquidity source
- payout conditions
- trading restrictions

Optional add-ons can modify some parameters, such as profit split and hedging rules.

### 4. Payment

Users pay a one-time fee for the selected account.

Supported payment methods:

- crypto
- card

After payment confirmation:

- the account is activated
- the user gains access to the trading platform

### 5. Trading

All trading happens inside the Doji platform.

Each account is assigned a virtual allocation used for internal accounting and risk logic.

That balance is not directly withdrawable.

Trading behavior is designed to mirror real market conditions.

Off-platform trading is not permitted.

### 6. Monitoring and verification

While trading, the platform tracks:

- positions
- PnL
- drawdowns
- rule compliance
- account status

Trade positions are recorded on-chain as NFTs with position metadata.

Performance data is visible in the dashboard and connected to the live NFT Explorer experience.

### 7. Payouts

When payout conditions are met, traders request payouts from the dashboard.

Performance is verified through platform infrastructure and payouts settle on-chain.

<Warning>
  Attempts to bypass platform rules, such as wash trading, prohibited strategies, or external execution, can invalidate the account.
</Warning>
