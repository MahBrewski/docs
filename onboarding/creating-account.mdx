Getting started takes a minute. Choose a login method, set your identity, and land in the terminal, ready to pick an account and trade.

## Sign up

| Method     | How it works                                                        |
| ---------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Email**  | Sign up with your email and confirm from the verification link.    |
| **Wallet** | Connect any supported EVM wallet to sign in without a password.    |

During signup, DojiFunded creates a dedicated **embedded wallet** for you, so no external wallet or browser extension is required. Set your trading identity with a username and avatar, which appear across the dashboard, leaderboard, and analytics. Then enter the terminal to browse account types, purchase an account, and trade.

## What links to your account

| Component            | Description                          |
| -------------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| **Email or wallet**  | Login, verification, and recovery    |
| **Trading identity** | Your username and public profile     |
| **Embedded wallet**  | Payments, trading, and payouts       |
| **Evaluations**      | Current and completed assessments    |
| **Funded accounts**  | Active funded accounts and history   |
| **Payouts**          | On-chain payout records              |
| **Analytics**        | Performance, PnL, and risk stats     |
