Create your DojiFunded account and start trading in under a minute.

## Before you start

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card icon="envelope" title="Email or wallet">An email address or wallet connection to sign up</Card>
  <Card icon="user" title="Trading identity">A username for your public trader profile</Card>
  <Card icon="coins" title="Account fee">Enough balance to cover your one-time evaluation or activation fee</Card>
</CardGroup>

## Create your account

<Steps>
  <Step title={<span style={{color: "#B7FF00"}}>Sign up</span>}>
    Create your account with email or wallet connection.

    During signup, DojiFunded automatically creates a dedicated embedded wallet for your account.
  </Step>

  <Step title={<span style={{color: "#B7FF00"}}>Set up your trading identity</span>}>
    Choose your username and profile avatar.

    Your trading identity is used across the dashboard, leaderboard, and trading analytics.
  </Step>

  <Step title={<span style={{color: "#B7FF00"}}>Select your account type</span>}>
    <CardGroup cols={2}>
      <Card icon="bolt" title="Instant Funding (coming soon)">Skip the evaluation — get funded immediately</Card>
      <Card icon="1" title="1-Step">Single evaluation phase to qualify for funding</Card>
      <Card icon="2" title="2-Step Elite">Two-phase evaluation with 6% static drawdown</Card>
      <Card icon="2" title="2-Step Classic">Two-phase evaluation with 8% static drawdown</Card>
    </CardGroup>
  </Step>

  <Step title={<span style={{color: "#B7FF00"}}>Complete payment</span>}>
    <CardGroup cols={2}>
      <Card icon="bitcoin-sign" title="Crypto">Pay using supported cryptocurrency</Card>
      <Card icon="credit-card" title="Card">Pay with debit or credit card</Card>
    </CardGroup>

    After payment confirmation, your account is activated and you can access the platform.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What gets linked to your account

| Component | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Email or wallet | Used for login, verification, and recovery |
| Trading identity | Your username and public trader profile |
| Embedded wallet | Used for platform payments, trading activity, and payouts |
| Assessments | Your current and completed evaluations |
| Funded accounts | Your active funded accounts and performance history |
| Payouts | Your on-chain payout history and settlement records |
| Trading analytics | Your performance metrics, PnL, and risk statistics |

## Your Doji wallet

<CardGroup cols={1}>
  <Card icon="plug" title="No external wallet required">No browser extension or third-party wallet setup needed. Your embedded wallet is built directly into the platform.</Card>
