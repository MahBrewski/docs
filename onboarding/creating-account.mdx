Create your DojiFunded account and start trading in under a minute.

## Before you start

You need:

- an email address or wallet connection
- a username for your trading identity
- enough balance to pay your one-time account or evaluation fee

## Create your account

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign up">
    Create your account with email or wallet connection.

    During signup, DojiFunded creates a dedicated embedded wallet for your account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up your trading identity">
    Choose your username and profile avatar.

    Your trading identity is used across the dashboard, leaderboard, and trading analytics.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select your account type">
    Choose from `Degen (Instant Funding)`, `1-Step`, `2-Step Elite`, or `2-Step Classic`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose an execution venue">
    Select the execution venue or execution partner for your account.

    Supported markets, liquidity conditions, and fees can vary by venue.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete payment">
    Pay the one-time account or evaluation fee with crypto or card.

    After payment confirmation, your account is activated and you can access the platform.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What gets linked to your account

| Component | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Email or wallet | Used for login, verification, and recovery |
| Trading identity | Your username and public trader profile |
| Embedded wallet | Used for platform payments, trading activity, and payouts |
| Assessments | Your current and completed evaluations |
| Funded accounts | Your active funded accounts and performance history |
| Payouts | Your on-chain payout history and settlement records |
| Trading analytics | Your performance metrics, PnL, and risk statistics |

## Your Doji wallet

Every DojiFunded account includes an embedded wallet.

- You do not need a browser extension to start using the platform.
- The wallet is used for payments, trading infrastructure, and payout settlement.
- Eligible payouts are processed to your embedded wallet through on-chain settlement.
