Inside the terminal, buying an account takes three steps. Everything settles in **USDC on Arbitrum**, though you can pay from any wallet, any chain, or by card.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select the type">
    Log in with the method you used at signup. Your embedded wallet loads automatically. Choose your account type.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose any add-ons">
    Adjust your profit split or risk limits at checkout.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Pay">
    Pay in USDC using whichever method is easiest. All three settle to your wallet as USDC on Arbitrum.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Account types

| Type                       | Description                                    |
| -------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- |
| **Instant Funding** (soon) | Skip the evaluation and get funded immediately |
| **1-Step**                 | Single evaluation to qualify                   |
| **2-Step Classic**         | Two-phase evaluation, 6% static drawdown       |
| **2-Step Elite**           | Two-phase evaluation, 8% static drawdown       |

## Payment methods

| Method               | How it works                                                                          |
| -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Crypto wallet**    | Scan the QR or copy your deposit address to send USDC, or connect a wallet for one-click pay. |
| **Card**             | Pay by debit or credit card; the purchase is on-ramped to USDC to activate the account. |
| **Cross-chain swap** | Convert any asset on any chain into USDC on Arbitrum via a swap provider.              |

<Warning>
  Direct crypto payments must be **USDC on Arbitrum**. Sending other tokens or using an unsupported network can result in lost funds. Moving funds from a different chain or asset? Use the cross-chain swap option instead.
</Warning>

Once your payment confirms, the account activates automatically and your evaluation goes live.

## Payment details

| Detail                | Value                                    |
| --------------------- | ---------------------------------------- |
| **Token**             | USDC                                     |
| **Network**           | Arbitrum                                 |
| **Confirmation time** | Typically under 30 seconds               |
| **Minimum amount**    | Enough to cover the account and add-ons  |
