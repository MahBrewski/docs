Before you can purchase an account, your embedded wallet must hold enough `USDC` to cover the account or evaluation fee.

## Supported funding methods

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card icon="credit-card" title="Card payments">Use a debit or credit card to instantly top up your DojiFunded wallet with USDC directly inside the platform</Card>
  <Card icon="bitcoin-sign" title="Crypto deposits">Send USDC from centralised exchanges, DeFi wallets, or on-chain bridges. Supported wallets include MetaMask, Rabby, Coinbase Wallet, and other EVM-compatible wallets</Card>
</CardGroup>

## Fund your wallet

<Steps>
  <Step title={<span style={{color: "#B7FF00"}}>Open the wallet flow</span>}>
    Open the wallet section from the dashboard or continue through the account checkout flow.
  </Step>

  <Step title={<span style={{color: "#B7FF00"}}>Add USDC</span>}>
    Choose your preferred funding method:

    <CardGroup cols={2}>
      <Card icon="credit-card" title="Card payment">Purchase USDC instantly — deposited into your Doji wallet after payment confirmation</Card>
      <Card icon="arrow-right-arrow-left" title="Crypto deposit">Send USDC over the supported network directly to your DojiFunded wallet address</Card>
    </CardGroup>
  </Step>

  <Step title={<span style={{color: "#B7FF00"}}>Start trading</span>}>
    Once your balance is confirmed, purchase your account and begin trading immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Only send supported assets on supported networks. Sending unsupported assets or using unsupported networks can result in lost funds.
</Warning>

## Deposit details

| Detail | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Token | USDC |
| Network | Supported EVM networks |
| Confirmation time | Typically under 30 seconds |
| Minimum deposit | No minimum — deposit enough to cover your account fee |
