Before you can purchase an account, your embedded wallet must hold enough `USDC` to cover the account or evaluation fee.

## Supported funding methods

You can fund your Doji wallet with:

- card payments
- crypto deposits

Card payments let you buy USDC inside the platform.

Crypto deposits let you transfer USDC from:

- centralized exchanges
- DeFi wallets
- on-chain bridges

Supported wallets include MetaMask, Rabby, Coinbase Wallet, and other EVM-compatible wallets.

## Fund your wallet

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the wallet flow">
    Open the wallet section from the dashboard or continue through the account checkout flow.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add USDC">
    Choose either a crypto deposit or a card payment.

    Card payments deposit USDC into your Doji wallet after payment confirmation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm your balance">
    Once your balance updates, you can purchase your account and begin trading.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Only send supported assets on supported networks. Sending unsupported assets or using unsupported networks can result in lost funds.
</Warning>

## Deposit details

| Detail | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Token | USDC |
| Network | Supported EVM networks |
| Confirmation time | Typically under 30 seconds |
| Minimum deposit | No minimum, as long as it covers your account fee |
