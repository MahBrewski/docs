## Market categories

This page reflects the current live market inventory returned by `https://oracle.dojifunded.com/v1/markets` as of May 9, 2026.

## How this page is divided

This market reference is divided two ways:

- by asset class
- by execution venue

DojiFunded currently exposes market identity and venue availability from the live oracle feed. Leverage limits, margin rules, and live position caps can change, so check the terminal and your account configuration before you trade.

## Why markets depend on venue

Available markets can vary by execution venue.

Your selected execution venue can affect:

- which pairs are listed
- liquidity conditions
- fee structure
- execution environment

## Current venue split

DojiFunded currently uses two execution venues:

| Venue | Main market coverage |
| --- | --- |
| GMX | Crypto and commodities |
| Ostium | Stocks, ETFs, commodities, indices, currencies, and selected crypto |

Markets available on both venues today: `ADA-USD`, `BNB-USD`, `BTC-USD`, `CVX-USD`, `ETH-USD`, `HYPE-USD`, `LINK-USD`, `SOL-USD`, `TRX-USD`, `XRP-USD`
