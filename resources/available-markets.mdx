DojiFunded is built for multi-asset market access from one trading platform.

## Market categories

DojiFunded currently supports or plans support for:

- crypto
- forex
- commodities
- stocks
- ETFs
- indices

Prediction markets and event-based products are described as planned rather than currently live.

## Why markets depend on venue

Available markets can vary by execution venue.

Your selected execution venue can affect:

- which pairs are listed
- liquidity conditions
- fee structure
- execution environment

## Current venue split

DojiFunded currently uses two execution venues:

| Venue | Main market coverage |
| --- | --- |
| GMX | Crypto and commodities |
| Ostium | Stocks, ETFs, commodities, indices, forex, and selected crypto |

## Example market coverage

Examples include:

- `GMX`: `BTCUSD`, `ETHUSD`, `SOLUSD`, `XRPUSD`, `HYPEUSD`, `GOLDUSD`, `WTIOILUSD`, `BRENTOILUSD`
- `Ostium`: `AAPL/USD`, `GOOG/USD`, `META/USD`, `MSFT/USD`, `NVDA/USD`, `US500/USD`, `US100/USD`, `EUR/USD`, `GBP/USD`, `BTC/USD`, `ETH/USD`

<Info>
  Market availability can change by venue. Check the terminal before relying on a specific instrument list.
</Info>

## Before you trade

Check the account setup and terminal market list before you place trades.

If a market is not available in your chosen venue, you may need to choose a different venue or account configuration.
