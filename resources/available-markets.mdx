## Market categories

This page reflects the go-live market set defined in the launch spreadsheet as of May 9, 2026.

## How this page is divided

This market reference is divided two ways:

- by asset class
- by execution venue

The tables below show only the pairs included in the go-live spreadsheet. If a pair exists elsewhere in internal tooling or the oracle feed but is not listed here, treat it as not part of the launch market set.

## Why markets depend on venue

Available markets can vary by execution venue.

Your selected execution venue can affect:

- which pairs are listed
- liquidity conditions
- fee structure
- execution environment

## Current venue split

DojiFunded currently uses two execution venues:

| Venue | Main market coverage |
| --- | --- |
| GMX | Crypto and commodities |
| Ostium | Stocks, ETFs, commodities, indices, currencies, and selected crypto |

Markets available on both venues in the go-live sheet: `ADA-USD`, `BNB-USD`, `BTC-USD`, `CVX-USD`, `ETH-USD`, `HYPE-USD`, `LINK-USD`, `SOL-USD`, `TRX-USD`, `XRP-USD`
