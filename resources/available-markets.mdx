Multi-asset market access from a single platform. This page lists the final go-live market set: 81 markets across crypto, forex, commodities, indices, and stocks.

Each market has its own maximum leverage and maximum position size. Both are enforced when you place the order, so a position that exceeds either limit is rejected rather than partially filled. If a pair exists in internal tooling but isn't listed here, treat it as outside the launch set.

| Venue  | Main coverage                                                       |
| ------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| GMX    | Crypto and commodities                                              |
| Ostium | Stocks, commodities, indices, currencies, and selected crypto       |

The terminal shows the execution venue for each market. Ten crypto pairs route to either venue at launch: ADA/USD, BNB/USD, BTC/USD, CVX/USD, ETH/USD, HYPE/USD, LINK/USD, SOL/USD, TRX/USD, XRP/USD.
