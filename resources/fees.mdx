DojiFunded currently documents a one-time account or evaluation fee during onboarding.

## What users pay for

Users pay a one-time fee based on:

- account type
- account configuration
- selected add-ons

The current product brief states there are no recurring fees for evaluations.

## What can vary

The shared brief also indicates that trading conditions can vary by execution venue.

That can include:

- fee structure
- funding costs
- execution conditions

## What to verify before publishing detailed fee docs

Before adding a detailed fee table, confirm:

- current account prices
- venue-level fee differences
- any funding or settlement costs visible to users
