DojiFunded accounts operate under predefined rules and risk parameters.

These rules define how evaluations, funded accounts, payout eligibility, and account breaches are handled.

## Core risk areas

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card icon="flag-checkered" title="Profit targets">Required balance growth to pass each evaluation phase</Card>
  <Card icon="triangle-exclamation" title="Max drawdown">Absolute equity floor — breaching it closes the account</Card>
  <Card icon="calendar-day" title="Daily drawdown">Maximum equity loss permitted within a single trading day</Card>
  <Card icon="sliders" title="Leverage limits">Maximum leverage per asset class and execution venue</Card>
  <Card icon="percent" title="Risk per trade">Maximum 1% risk per trade unless add-on is enabled</Card>
  <Card icon="clock" title="Activity requirement">At least one trade every 30 days to stay active</Card>
  <Card icon="arrows-left-right" title="Hedging rules">Hedging on the same currency is off by default</Card>
  <Card icon="ban" title="Copy trading">Copy trading between different users is not permitted</Card>
  <Card icon="stopwatch" title="Minimum trade duration">All trades must be open for at least 60 seconds</Card>
  <Card icon="circle-xmark" title="Account arbitrage">Cross-account arbitrage strategies are not allowed</Card>
</CardGroup>

## Account state and breaches

A breach occurs when the active drawdown threshold is exceeded or platform rules are violated.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card icon="lock" title="Trading access revoked">All order entry is blocked immediately upon breach</Card>
  <Card icon="circle-xmark" title="Payout eligibility removed">Breached accounts lose all payout rights</Card>
  <Card icon="power-off" title="Account becomes inactive">Breached accounts cannot be restored</Card>
  <Card icon="right-from-bracket" title="Positions managed">Open positions may be closed automatically at breach</Card>
</CardGroup>

## Trading requirements

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card icon="circle-check" title="No consistency rule">No daily profit caps or restrictions on single-trade profits</Card>
  <Card icon="calendar-xmark" title="No minimum trading days">Evaluations can be passed in a single trade if all rules are respected</Card>
  <Card icon="rotate" title="30-day activity">At least one trade every 30 days to keep the account active</Card>
  <Card icon="robot" title="Autonomous strategies">Algorithmic and AI-driven strategies are permitted</Card>
</CardGroup>

## Restricted behavior

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card icon="arrows-left-right" title="Cross-account hedging">Opposing positions across multiple accounts to guarantee profit on one</Card>
  <Card icon="copy" title="Copy trading between users">Mirroring positions or using shared signal groups across accounts</Card>
  <Card icon="user-slash" title="Account sharing">Sharing login credentials or trading another user's account</Card>
  <Card icon="bolt" title="Latency or pricing exploits">Exploiting execution delays, desync, or oracle pricing errors</Card>
  <Card icon="arrows-rotate" title="Artificial volume">Wash trading, self-matching, or meaningless trade loops</Card>
  <Card icon="chart-line" title="Market manipulation">Spoofing, coordinated attacks, or intentional liquidity disruption</Card>
</CardGroup>

<Warning>
  Violations may result in immediate account termination, payout cancellation, and permanent platform bans.
</Warning>

## Platform enforcement

Rule compliance is checked continuously. Drawdowns, trade activity, and account state are monitored in real time.

<Warning>
  Attempts to bypass platform rules — including wash trading, exploit attempts, prohibited coordination, or external execution — can invalidate the account.
</Warning>

## Payout rule concepts

<CardGroup cols={1}>
  <Card icon="flag-checkered">Funded accounts do not carry new profit targets after qualification</Card>
  <Card icon="layout-dashboard">Payout requests are made from the dashboard</Card>
  <Card icon="link">Eligible payouts settle on-chain to the embedded wallet</Card>
  <Card icon="sliders">Add-ons can affect the profit split percentage</Card>
</CardGroup>
