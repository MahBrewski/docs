DojiFunded accounts operate under predefined rules and risk parameters.

These rules define how evaluations, funded accounts, payout eligibility, and account breaches are handled.

## Core risk areas

Depending on account type, users should expect rules around:

- profit targets
- max drawdown
- daily drawdown
- leverage
- risk per trade
- activity requirements
- hedging permissions
- copy trading restrictions
- minimum trade duration
- account arbitrage restrictions

## Account state and breaches

Accounts can be breached if the active drawdown threshold is exceeded or if platform rules are violated.

When an account is breached, users should expect consequences such as:

- trading access being revoked
- payout eligibility being removed
- the account becoming inactive
- open positions being managed by platform controls where required

## Trading requirements

The current product materials position DojiFunded as:

- having no consistency rule
- having no minimum trading days requirement
- allowing evaluation completion in a single trade if all rules are respected
- requiring at least one trade every 30 days to keep an account active

The supported style set is broad and includes discretionary trading, algorithmic trading, and autonomous strategies, provided the account remains within platform rules.

## Restricted behavior

The user-facing rule set prohibits behavior intended to bypass account rules or distort platform outcomes.

This includes categories such as:

- cross-account hedging
- copy trading between different users
- account sharing
- latency or pricing exploit attempts
- artificial volume generation
- market manipulation
- strategies designed purely to exploit evaluation mechanics

## Why these rules matter

The platform uses these parameters to:

- evaluate trader performance
- enforce funded account eligibility
- determine whether an account remains in good standing

## Platform enforcement

Rule compliance is checked continuously.

Users should assume that drawdowns, trade activity, and account state are monitored in real time.

<Warning>
  Attempts to bypass platform rules, including wash trading, exploit attempts, prohibited coordination, or external execution, can invalidate the account.
</Warning>

## Payout rule concepts

Payout eligibility depends on account state, rule compliance, and the conditions for the selected account type.

The shared source material also states that:

- funded accounts do not carry new profit targets after qualification
- payout requests are made from the dashboard
- eligible payouts settle on-chain to the embedded wallet
- add-ons can affect the profit split

## Venue and fee dependencies

Some rule outcomes depend on the selected execution venue and market.

Users should confirm:

- the available market set for the venue
- leverage and position constraints
- trading fees
- any venue-level funding or rollover costs
