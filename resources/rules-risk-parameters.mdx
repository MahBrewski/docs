DojiFunded accounts operate under predefined rules and risk parameters.

These rules are part of the funded trading model and determine whether an account stays eligible.

## Core risk areas

Depending on account type, users should expect rules around:

- profit targets
- max drawdown
- daily drawdown
- leverage
- risk per trade
- activity requirements
- hedging permissions
- copy trading restrictions
- minimum trade duration
- account arbitrage restrictions

## Why these rules matter

The platform uses these parameters to:

- evaluate trader performance
- enforce funded account eligibility
- determine whether an account remains in good standing

## Platform enforcement

The product brief and frontend both indicate that rule compliance is checked continuously.

Users should assume that drawdowns, trade activity, and account state are monitored in real time.

<Warning>
  Attempts to bypass platform rules, including wash trading, prohibited strategies, or external execution, can invalidate the account.
</Warning>
