An execution venue is the trading environment or partner protocol behind your account.

You choose your execution venue during account setup.

## What the venue affects

Your venue can determine:

- supported trading pairs
- liquidity conditions
- fee structure
- funding costs
- execution environment

## Current venues

DojiFunded currently supports:

- `GMX`
- `Ostium`

All funded trades are described as routing through supported on-chain execution infrastructure.

## Why venue selection matters

Venue selection is part of the account setup flow, not an afterthought.

It can change the practical trading experience for the same account type.

## Venue summary

### GMX

`GMX` supports on-chain crypto and commodity execution.

Examples listed there include:

- crypto perpetual markets such as `BTCUSD`, `ETHUSD`, `SOLUSD`, `XRPUSD`, and `HYPEUSD`
- commodity markets such as `GOLDUSD`, `WTIOILUSD`, `BRENTOILUSD`, `SILVERUSD`, and `NATGASUSD`

### Ostium

`Ostium` supports broader multi-asset execution.

Examples listed there include:

- stocks such as `AAPL/USD`, `GOOG/USD`, `META/USD`, `MSFT/USD`, and `NVDA/USD`
- ETFs such as `TLT/USD`, `XLE/USD`, `HYG/USD`, `UNG/USD`, and `URA/USD`
- indices such as `US500/USD`, `US100/USD`, `US30/USD`, `GER40/EUR`, and `UK100/GBP`
- forex such as `EUR/USD`, `GBP/USD`, `USD/JPY`, `AUD/USD`, and `USD/CAD`
- selected crypto such as `BTC/USD`, `ETH/USD`, `SOL/USD`, `XRP/USD`, and `HYPE/USD`

## What is still future-facing

Prediction markets and additional execution venues are planned.

Do not present them as live until the product surface confirms them.

## Availability note

Market coverage and venue limits can change over time. Confirm the terminal market list before relying on a specific pair or limit.
