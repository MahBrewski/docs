An execution venue is the trading environment behind your account. You select it during account setup.

## What the venue determines

Your venue determines:

* Which pairs you can trade
* The liquidity
* The fees
* The funding costs
* The execution environment

All funded trades route through supported on-chain execution.

Venue selection is part of account setup, not an afterthought. The same account type can trade quite differently depending on the venue you choose.

## Venue summary

**GMX.** On-chain crypto and commodities. Examples: BTCUSD, ETHUSD, SOLUSD, XRPUSD, HYPEUSD, GOLDUSD, WTIOILUSD, BRENTOILUSD, SILVERUSD, NATGASUSD.

**Ostium.** Broader and multi-asset:

* **Stocks** such as AAPL/USD, GOOG/USD, META/USD, MSFT/USD, NVDA/USD
* **ETFs** such as TLT/USD, XLE/USD, HYG/USD
* **Indices** such as US500/USD, US100/USD, GER40/EUR
* **Forex** such as EUR/USD, GBP/USD, AUD/USD
* **Crypto** such as BTC/USD, ETH/USD, SOL/USD

<Note>
  Prediction markets and additional venues are planned, and should not be treated as live until confirmed. Coverage and limits change over time, so verify the terminal market list before relying on a specific pair or limit.
</Note>
