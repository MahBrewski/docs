## Partner rewards structure

Five tiers, set by how many active users you onboard. The more you bring in, the more you earn on every purchase.

* **Tier 1 (0-20 users):** 10% on first purchase, 5% on subsequent purchases.
* **Tier 2 (20-60 users):** 15% on first purchase, 5% on subsequent purchases.
* **Tier 3 (60-120 users):** 15% on first purchase, 7.5% on subsequent purchases.
* **Tier 4 (120-240 users):** 20% on first purchase, 7.5% on subsequent purchases.
* **Tier 5 (240+ users):** 20% on first purchase, 10% on subsequent purchases.

Every tier also earns a **10% lifetime commission** on your partners' profits on active funded accounts, and everyone you refer gets a **10% discount** on their account.
