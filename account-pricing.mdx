## Add-ons

DojiFunded offers optional add-ons during checkout.

Each add-on increases the base account price by `20%`.

| Add-on | What it changes |
| --- | --- |
| `80% / 90% Profit Split` | Increases the profit split on eligible accounts |
| `Remove 1% Risk Per Trade Limit` | Removes the maximum `1%` risk-per-trade restriction |

## Pricing examples

Use the base price, then add `20%` for each add-on you select.

| Base price | Add-ons selected | Final price |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `$100` | None | `$100` |
| `$100` | One add-on | `$120` |
| `$100` | Two add-ons | `$140` |

## What affects pricing

DojiFunded pricing is based on:

- funding access
- evaluation difficulty
- drawdown structure
- payout risk
- operational and risk infrastructure

Accounts with faster funding access or lower evaluation friction are priced higher because they expose more capital earlier in the account lifecycle.
