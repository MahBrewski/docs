Your price comes down to three things: **account type**, **account size**, and **any add-ons**. A single fee, paid once, with nothing recurring.

## Base prices

| Account size | Instant Funding *(soon)* | 1-Step | 2-Step Classic | 2-Step Elite |
| ------------ | ------------------------ | ------ | -------------- | ------------ |
| $1,000       | $30                      | $13    | $10            | $12          |
| $5,000       | $146                     | $51    | $42            | $50          |
| $10,000      | $284                     | $93    | $84            | $100         |
| $25,000      | $719                     | $231   | $210           | $250         |
| $50,000      | $1,439                   | $416   | $380           | $450         |
| $100,000     | not offered              | $840   | $752           | $900         |

## Add-on cost

Each add-on raises the base price by **20%**.

```
Final price = Base price × (1 + 0.20 × number of add-ons)
```

| Base price | Add-ons | Final price |
| ---------- | ------- | ----------- |
| $100       | none    | $100        |
| $100       | one     | $120        |
| $100       | two     | $140        |

## What drives price

DojiFunded pricing reflects:

* Funding access
* Evaluation difficulty
* Drawdown structure
* Payout risk
* Operational and risk infrastructure

Accounts with faster funding access or lower evaluation friction are priced higher, because they expose more capital earlier in the account lifecycle.
