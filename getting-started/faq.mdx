## Evaluations & Risk

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How do evaluations work?">
    Traders can purchase their preferred evaluation challenge with preferred add-ons directly from the dashboard and begin trading immediately.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What is Trailing Drawdown?">
    Trailing drawdown follows your account's highest equity value and moves up as you make profits — it never moves back down.

    **Example:**
    - You start with a $100,000 account and a 6% drawdown.
    - Your minimum equity starts at **$94,000**.
    - You grow the account to $105,000.
    - Your minimum equity becomes **$99,000**.
    - If the account drops below that level, the challenge fails.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What is Static Drawdown?">
    Static drawdown stays fixed from the starting balance — it does not move higher when your account grows.

    **Example:**
    - You start with a $100,000 account and a 6% drawdown.
    - Your minimum equity is **$94,000**.
    - Even if you grow the account to $120,000, the limit stays the same.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Are drawdowns equity-based?">
    No. Drawdowns are based on your **realised account balance**, not floating PnL. Open trades do not affect drawdown calculations until positions are closed.

    Daily drawdown is calculated using a high-water mark (HWM), while maximum drawdown depends on your account type.

    **Example — Daily Drawdown (HWM):**
    - You start the day with a balance of $100,000 and a 5% daily loss limit, meaning your maximum daily loss is **$5,000**.
    - During the day, your balance reaches $105,000. Your daily drawdown floor becomes **$100,000** ($105,000 − $5,000).
    - If your balance later drops to $99,500, the account fails because the realised balance fell below the daily drawdown floor.

    The formula is always:

    **Daily HWM Floor = Highest Balance of the Day − (Daily Loss % × Initial Account Size)**
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What happens if I break a rule?">
    If your account breaches a daily or maximum drawdown limit, it fails automatically. There is no manual review.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is there a time limit to pass?">
    Most evaluations do not have a deadline — you can pass in a day or take your time. Accounts with 30 days of inactivity may be paused.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I have multiple accounts?">
    Yes. You can run multiple evaluations simultaneously. There is no cap on how many accounts a user can trade with.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Trading & Execution

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Is slippage included?">
    No. Slippage is not included — you are only subject to opening/closing fees and funding fees. On moving to the funded phase, the Doji internal risk engine absorbs slippage when executing on real venues.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What order types are supported?">
    DojiFunded supports market orders, limit orders, stop market, and stop limit orders.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Are trading fees charged?">
    Depending on the market, trades may include opening fees, closing fees, funding fees, and rollover fees. All fees are visible before placing a trade.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Are trading bots allowed?">
    Yes. DojiFunded supports algorithmic and automated trading strategies. Strategies that abuse latency, pricing errors, or platform mechanics are not allowed and are subject to account bans.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
