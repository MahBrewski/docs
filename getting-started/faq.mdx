## Evaluations & Risk

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How do evaluations work?">
    Traders purchase challenge accounts and trade within predefined drawdown and risk limits to qualify for funding.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What is Smart Drawdown?">
    Smart Drawdown trails account equity until a target threshold is reached, after which the drawdown floor locks permanently.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What is Trailing Drawdown?">
    Trailing Drawdown follows your account high-water mark upward and never moves back down.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What is Static Drawdown?">
    Static Drawdown remains fixed from the initial account balance regardless of profits or account growth.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Are drawdowns equity-based?">
    Yes. All drawdowns are calculated using real-time equity including floating PnL from open positions.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What happens if I breach a rule?">
    Accounts that violate daily or maximum drawdown rules are automatically locked.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is there a time limit to pass?">
    Most evaluations do not require traders to pass within a fixed deadline.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I have multiple accounts?">
    Yes. Traders may operate multiple evaluations simultaneously within platform limits.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Trading & Execution

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How is execution handled?">
    Orders use a synthetic execution engine optimized for low slippage, smooth fills, and realistic market impact.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is there slippage?">
    Yes. Execution includes base spreads and dynamic market impact depending on trade size and market conditions.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Are trading fees charged?">
    Trades may incur opening fees, closing fees, funding fees, or rollover costs depending on the asset.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Are leverage limits applied?">
    Yes. Leverage limits vary by asset class to maintain fair risk conditions and platform stability.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Are bots or automation allowed?">
    Automation is supported, but exploitative strategies such as latency arbitrage or oracle abuse may be restricted.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
