## Evaluations & Risk

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How do evaluations work?">
    Traders purchase their preferred evaluation challenge with any desired add-ons from the dashboard and begin trading immediately. There is no waiting period.

    1. Choose a challenge size and plan type
    2. Optionally add add-ons (e.g. 90% profit split)
    3. Pay with USDC on Arbitrum and start trading
    4. Hit the profit target while staying within drawdown and rule limits
    5. Pass and become eligible for a funded account
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What is Trailing Drawdown?">
    Trailing drawdown follows your account's highest equity value and moves up as you make profits — it never moves back down. This makes it progressively harder to lose back gains.

    **Example:**
    - Start: $10,000 — trailing amount: $600
    - Account hits $11,500 → drawdown floor moves to $10,900
    - Lose $300 → balance $11,200, still safe
    - Lose further → floor does not drop back down
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What is Static Drawdown?">
    Static drawdown stays fixed from your starting balance and never moves — even if your account grows. This gives you more room to take risks early since profits don't tighten the floor.

    **Example:**
    - Start: $10,000 — drawdown floor: $9,200
    - After profit: balance $11,000 — floor still $9,200
    - After loss: balance $9,500 — still safe (above $9,200)

    Note: daily drawdown still updates each day based on the high-water mark.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What is Smart Drawdown?">
    Smart Drawdown is a hybrid model combining static and trailing systems. It starts at -6% and automatically trails to 0% once you achieve a 6% gain on your account balance.

    **Initial Phase**

    Your account begins with a -6% drawdown from the starting balance. No time limits — trade at your own pace.
    - Starting balance: $10,000
    - Initial drawdown limit: $9,400

    **Profit Phase**

    Once you hit a 6% gain, the drawdown adjusts to 0% of starting balance and locks permanently there.
    - After 10% gain: balance $11,000 → drawdown locks at $10,000
    - That $10,000 limit stays fixed regardless of future profits

    **Key Benefits**
    - Clear, predictable drawdown levels
    - No time limits
    - Faster progression to larger accounts
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Are drawdowns equity-based?">
    Drawdowns are based on **realised account balance**, not floating PnL. Open positions do not count toward drawdown until they are closed.

    Daily drawdown uses a high-water mark calculation — it resets at 23:00 UTC each day based on whichever is higher between your balance and equity at that moment.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How Daily Loss is calculated?">
    Each account type has its own daily loss limit, recalculated at 23:00 UTC every day.

    | Account Type | Daily Loss Limit |
    |---|---|
    | One Phase | 3% |
    | Two Phase Classic | 3% |
    | Two Phase Elite | 5% |

    **Balance vs Equity**

    The higher value between Balance and Equity at 23:00 UTC is used to set the daily floor.

    - Balance at reset: $10,200 — Equity at reset: $10,350
    - $10,350 is used → 3% of $10,000 = $300 → floor set at $9,700 equity
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What happens if I break a rule?">
    If your account breaches a daily or maximum drawdown limit, it fails automatically. There is no manual review — the system is fully automated and on-chain.

    For certain rule violations (like HFT), the first two instances result in a warning and profit deduction. A third instance leads to an account breach.

    After a breach, you can purchase a new account or, if eligible, use a reset code. Program and reset rates may change over time.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is there a time limit?">
    Most evaluations do not have a deadline — trade at your own pace. However, accounts that are inactive for 30 or more days may be paused.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I retry my account?">
    Yes. If you fail a challenge, you can reset your evaluation at a special rate. If you lose a funded account, we automatically send you a code to help you restart.

    Note that program and reset rates may change over time.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Trading & Execution

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Are trades executed on live markets?">
    During the evaluation phase, trading is conducted in a professional virtual environment using real-time market data — accurate price movements, spreads, and market depth that match actual markets.

    After reaching the funded phase, trades are executed on real markets, which you can verify on-chain through the DojiFunded Explorer (for eligible accounts).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What order types are supported?">
    DojiFunded supports the following order types:

    - Market orders
    - Limit orders
    - Stop market orders
    - Stop limit orders
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Are there trading fees?">
    Possible fees include:

    - Opening fees
    - Closing fees
    - Funding fees
    - Rollover fees

    All fees are visible before you place a trade — no hidden charges.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Are trading bots allowed?">
    DojiFunded supports algorithmic and automated trading strategies. You are free to use your own custom bots and EAs.

    However, exploitative tactics are prohibited and may result in an account ban.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is News Trading allowed?">
    Yes, news trading is allowed on our platform.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is high frequency trading (HFT) allowed?">
    High frequency trading is not permitted. HFT strategies can create unfair advantages that go against our commitment to fair trading.

    **What counts as HFT?**
    - Trades held for 60 seconds or less
    - An unusually high number of trades per hour

    **How violations are handled**
    - 1st & 2nd instance: Warning + deduction of net profits from HFT trades
    - 3rd instance: Account breach

    Only profitable trades held for 60 seconds or less trigger violations. Losses within 60 seconds do not result in warnings.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Are there any prohibited trading practices?">
    To ensure fairness and platform integrity, the following are not allowed:

    **Unethical Trading**
    - **Latency Arbitrage:** Exploiting data update delays for unfair advantage
    - **Tick Scalping:** Profiting from minimal price fluctuations at unsustainable speeds
    - **Account Arbitrage:** Using multiple accounts to exploit differences in conditions or risk limits

    **Reverse Trading & Group Hedging**
    - **Reverse Trading:** Taking opposing positions on the same asset across accounts
    - **Group Hedging:** Coordinating positions across multiple traders to hedge risk

    **Group Copying & Account Management**
    - No copying trades between accounts using identical EA settings
    - No trading on behalf of others (violates KYC/AML rules)

    **Third Party EAs**

    Publicly available third-party Expert Advisors, algorithms, or bots are not permitted. Only first-party automated tools are supported.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is copy trading allowed?">
    You may copy trades from your own personal accounts at other prop firms or brokers to your DojiFunded account — as long as all accounts are owned by you.

    **Restrictions**
    - Same owner accounts only — no copying from friends, relatives, or others
    - No coordinated trading between separate traders (same IP address included)
    - No reverse trading or collusion strategies

    Accounts found in violation will be breached without refund.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Am I able to take hedging positions on multiple accounts?">
    No. Taking opposite positions on multiple accounts is considered cheating and is strictly prohibited. Accounts found doing this will be closed without a refund.

    **Example:** Opening a long on BTCUSD on one account and a short on BTCUSD on another account constitutes prohibited opposite positioning.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

  <Accordion title="Key terms explained">
    **Account Values**

    - **Balance:** The total value of completed trades, deposits, and withdrawals. Does not include open position PnL.
    - **Equity:** Real-time account value — balance plus unrealized PnL from open positions.

    **Risk Management**

    - **Max Drawdown:** A hard limit on total account losses. If equity drops below this level, trading is suspended.
    - **Daily Drawdown:** A per-day loss limit measured from the high-water mark at 23:00 UTC.
    - **Smart Drawdown:** Starts at -6% and trails to 0% once a 6% gain is achieved. Then locks permanently.
    - **Breach:** When a trading rule is violated, leading to automatic account deactivation.
