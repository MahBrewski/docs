## Evaluations & Risk

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How do evaluations work?">
    Traders can purchase their preferred evaluation challenge with preferred add-ons directly from the dashboard and begin trading immediately.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What is Static Drawdown?">
    Static drawdown stays fixed from the starting balance, it does not move higher when your account grows.

    **Example**

    You start with a $100,000 account and a 6% drawdown.

    Your minimum equity is: $94,000

    Even if you grow the account to $120,000, the limit stays the same.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Are drawdowns equity-based?">
    Drawdowns are calculated on your high water mark (HWM): whichever is higher of your equity and balance counts. Open trades affect your drawdown in real time, since equity moves with them.

    The daily HWM resets every day at 00:00 UTC. Maximum drawdown depends on your account type.

    **Example of Daily Drawdown (HWM)**

    You start the day with an account balance of: $100,000

    Your daily loss limit is 5% of account size. This means your maximum daily loss is: $5,000

    During the day, your account reaches $105,000, in balance or equity.

    Your daily drawdown floor now becomes: $100,000 ($105,000 − $5,000)

    Your account later drops to: $99,500

    The account fails because it fell below the daily drawdown floor.

    **Another example makes it even clearer:**

    If the account reaches: $110,000

    Your daily floor becomes: $105,000 ($110,000 − $5,000)

    So the formula is always:

    Daily HWM Floor = Day's High Water Mark − (Daily Loss % × Initial Account Size)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What happens if I break a rule?">
    If your account breaches a daily or maximum drawdown limit, it fails automatically.

    There's no manual review.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is there a time limit?">
    Most evaluations do not have a deadline. You can pass in a day or take your time.

    Accounts with 30 days of inactivity may be paused.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I have multiple accounts?">
    Yes, You can run multiple evaluations within platform limits as there is no cap on how many accounts a user can trade with.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Trading & Execution

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Is slippage included?">
    No, slippage is not included and a user will be subjected to only opening/closing fees and funding fees.

    On moving to the funded phase, the Doji internal risk engine absorbs slippage fee when being executed on real execution venues.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What order types are supported?">
    Dojifunded supports market orders, limit orders, stop market and stop limit orders.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Are there trading fees?">
    Depending on the market, trades may include:

    - Opening fees
    - Closing fees
    - Funding fees
    - Rollover fees

    Fees are visible before placing a trade.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Are Trading bots allowed?">
    Yes, Dojifunded has provisions to support Algorithmic and automated trading strategies.

    Strategies that abuse latency, pricing errors, or platform mechanics are not allowed and are subjected to account bans.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Are they any timelimits per trade?">
    Every trade/position opened on Dojifunded has to be held for a minimum of 60 seconds before it can be closed to prevent gaming and HFT strategies being run on the platform.

    This applies to TP/SL as well. Even if your set TP/SL closes the trade within 60 seconds, it will still be considered a violation of the rule.

    We understand that sometimes a trade can get closed by mistake, so every trader gets two warnings before a breach. The third time a trade gets closed within 60 seconds, it will lead to a breach.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
