DojiFunded offers four account types with funding access from `$1K` to `$100K`.

Each account type is designed for a different trading style and risk profile.

## What stays true across plans

These cross-plan rules apply across the current account lineup:

- no consistency rule
- no minimum trading days requirement
- no recurring evaluation fee
- at least one trade every 30 days to keep the account active

## How to choose an account

Choose based on:

- whether you want an evaluation phase or instant funding
- how strict you want the drawdown model to be
- how you want payouts and progression to work
- whether your strategy fits the account's daily drawdown and risk limits

## Add-ons

DojiFunded offers optional add-ons that let you customize your account:

- `80% / 90% Profit Split`
- `Remove 1% Risk Per Trade Limit`
- `Enable Hedging on the Same Currency`

Each add-on increases the base account price by `20%`.

<Note>
  Add-ons modify specific account parameters. Review the final account setup before you complete payment.
</Note>

<Warning>
  `Enable Hedging on the Same Currency` is currently marked as coming soon in account setup. Do not treat it as universally available until the purchase flow exposes it as selectable.
</Warning>

## Rule model

DojiFunded positions these accounts as transparent and rule-based.

DojiFunded emphasizes:

- no hidden rules
- clear drawdown models
- explicit activity requirements
- defined payout conditions
- platform-enforced breaches when rules are exceeded
