DojiFunded offers four account types with funding access from `$1K` to `$100K`.

Each account type is designed for a different trading style and risk profile.

## Account types

| Rule | Degen (Instant Funding) | 1-Step | 2-Step Elite | 2-Step Classic |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Funding model | Instant funding | Single evaluation | Two-phase evaluation | Two-phase evaluation |
| Profit target | None | 10% | 10% in step 1, 5% in step 2 | 10% in step 1, 5% in step 2 |
| First or minimum withdrawal | First withdrawal after 5% profit | Minimum 1% of account size | Minimum 1% of account size | Minimum 1% of account size |
| Max drawdown | 5% trailing drawdown | 6% smart drawdown | 6% static drawdown | 8% static drawdown |
| Daily drawdown | None | 3% | 3% | 5% |
| Leverage | Up to 5x crypto, 10x indices, commodities and stocks, 25x forex, 20x ETFs | Up to 5x crypto, 10x indices, commodities and stocks, 25x forex, 20x ETFs | Up to 5x crypto, 10x indices, commodities and stocks, 25x forex, 20x ETFs | Up to 5x crypto, 10x indices, commodities and stocks, 25x forex, 20x ETFs |
| Risk per trade | Max 1% | Max 1% | Max 1% | Max 1% |
| Activity requirement | At least 1 trade every 30 days | At least 1 trade every 30 days | At least 1 trade every 30 days | At least 1 trade every 30 days |
| Copy trading | Not allowed between users | Not allowed between users | Not allowed between users | Not allowed between users |
| Minimum trade duration | 60 seconds | 60 seconds | 60 seconds | 60 seconds |
| Account arbitrage | Not allowed | Not allowed | Not allowed | Not allowed |
| Time limit | None | None | None | None |

## What stays true across plans

These cross-plan rules apply across the current account lineup:

- no consistency rule
- no minimum trading days requirement
- no recurring evaluation fee
- at least one trade every 30 days to keep the account active

## How to choose an account

Choose based on:

- whether you want an evaluation phase or instant funding
- how strict you want the drawdown model to be
- how you want payouts and progression to work
- whether your strategy fits the account's daily drawdown and risk limits

## Add-ons

DojiFunded offers optional add-ons that let you customize your account:

- `80% / 90% Profit Split`
- `Remove 1% Risk Per Trade Limit`

Each add-on increases the base account price by `20%`.

<Note>
  Degen (Instant Funding) accounts are listed in the current account model but are still marked as coming soon in the product flow. Review the final account setup at checkout before you complete payment.
</Note>

## Pricing

Pricing depends on your account type, account size, and any add-ons you enable at checkout.

Review [account pricing](/account-pricing) before you purchase so you can compare base prices across the full account lineup.


## Rule model

DojiFunded positions these accounts as transparent and rule-based.

DojiFunded emphasizes:

- no hidden rules
- clear drawdown models
- explicit activity requirements
- defined payout conditions
- platform-enforced breaches when rules are exceeded
