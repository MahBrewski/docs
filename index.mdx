DojiFunded is an on-chain funded trading protocol where traders prove their performance, get funded up to **\$100K**, and earn up to **90%** of profits.

Dojifunded brings prop trading into a single on-chain platform. With a custom proprietary trading terminal, risk execution stack and verifiable on-chain proof for every account proof. From evaluations and funding to execution and payouts, every step happens within one transparent, on-chain ecosystem.

## How Prop Trading works?

* You pay a one-time account or evaluation fee.
* You trade under defined rules and drawdown limits.
* You qualify for funding by meeting the required targets for your account type.
* You request payouts and keep a majority share of the profits.

## Traditional exchange vs DojiFunded

On a traditional exchange, your own capital is on the line. On DojiFunded, you trade a platform-provided allocation under fixed rules and earn a share of what you make.

|                      | Traditional exchange           | DojiFunded                                  |
| -------------------- | ------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------- |
| **Your capital**     | You trade your own funds       | You trade platform-provided capital         |
| **Risk of loss**     | Funds fully exposed to losses  | Risk capped at the account fee              |
| **Requirements**     | Can be steep                   | Low upfront cost, scalable buying power     |
| **Where profit sits**| Your personal account          | Up to 90% profit share from funded accounts |
| **Execution**        | Often opaque                   | On-chain and verifiable                     |

Two ideas run through everything below.

* **Static drawdown.** A fixed floor set from your starting balance that never moves as the account grows.
* **High water mark.** Drawdown limits are measured on HWM basis, whichever is higher of your balance and equity, and the daily mark resets at 00:00 UTC.

Keep both in mind and the rest of the rulebook follows naturally.
