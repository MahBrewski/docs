DojiFunded uses a transparent funded trading model from onboarding to payout.

## The funded trading flow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start an evaluation or instant funding account">
    Choose your account type, account size, and execution venue.

    You trade within predefined risk parameters and work toward the required target for your plan.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Trade under platform rules">
    Use the trading terminal to manage positions, monitor PnL, and stay within drawdown and rule limits.

    Rule compliance is monitored continuously while you trade.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get funded">
    After you meet the conditions for your account type, you move into the funded stage.

    Funded accounts continue to operate under defined risk parameters.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Request payouts">
    When your account becomes eligible, request payouts from the dashboard.

    Payouts are verified and settled through on-chain infrastructure.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Scale your capital">
    Consistent performance can unlock larger account sizes, better payout terms, and future capital allocation opportunities.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What happens after funding

After you get funded, the platform does not add new profit targets to the funded stage.

You continue trading while staying inside the active risk parameters for your account.

## What you choose up front

Before you start trading, you choose:

- your account type
- your account size
- your execution venue
- any optional add-ons that modify account parameters

These choices define the risk model, payout conditions, and trading rules that apply to your account.

## What DojiFunded verifies

DojiFunded verifies:

- account performance
- drawdown and risk rule compliance
- payout eligibility
- trade activity through platform infrastructure

<Info>
  The platform is built for transparent, rule-based funded trading. If you need the specific parameters for each plan, compare the available account types before you purchase.
</Info>
