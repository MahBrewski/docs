One transparent flow, from onboarding to payout. Four stages, nothing hidden in between.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start an evaluation">
    Choose your account type, size, and any add-ons. Trade within your risk parameters and work toward the profit target for your plan.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Trade under platform rules">
    Manage positions, monitor PnL, and stay inside your drawdown and rule limits. Compliance is enforced automatically and continuously while you trade.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Get funded">
    Meet the conditions for your account type and move to the funded stage. Funded accounts trade under the same risk parameters, with no profit target.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Request payouts">
    Once eligible, request payouts from the dashboard. Verified and settled on-chain, instantly.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What you choose up front

Three choices fix your risk model, payout conditions, and trading rules for the life of the account.

| Choice           | Options                                                          |
| ---------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Account type** | Instant Funding (coming soon), 1-Step, 2-Step Elite, 2-Step Classic |
| **Account size** | \$1K to \$100K                                                  |
| **Add-ons**      | Optional adjustments to profit split and risk limits            |
